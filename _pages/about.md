Hello! My name is Lin Li (李淋), pronounced alike Lynn Lee. I am a postdoctoral researcher, advised by Prof. Yarin Gal, in the [Oxford Applied and Theoretical Machine Learning (OATML)](https://oatml.cs.ox.ac.uk/) Group at the Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford.

I received my PhD from King’s College London, supervised by Prof. [Michael Spratling](https://mwspratling.codeberg.page/), an MSc in Computing from Imperial College London under Prof. [Wayne Luk](http://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~wl/), and a B.M. in Finance from Xiamen University, advised by Prof. [Zheng Qiao](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/web/qiaozheng).

My research interests include

* Hallucination in (multimodal) LLMs
* LLM Jailbreaking, Safety Alignment, and Adversarial ML
* AI Society and LLM Agent
* AI+ Applications: healthcare, robotics, business
