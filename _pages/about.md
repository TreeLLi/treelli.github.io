Hello! I'm an AI researcher in the [OATML](https://oatml.cs.ox.ac.uk/) Group at the Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford, working with Prof. [Yarin Gal](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/yarin.gal/website/). I am also a Junior Research Fellow at [Jesus College](https://www.jesus.ox.ac.uk/), Oxford (since October 2026). I lead a research stream on multimodal foundation model safety within the Horizon Europe project, [DVPS](https://dvps.translated.com/). I am a Co-Principal Investigator on a research grant from [Coefficient Giving](https://coefficientgiving.org/funds/navigating-transformative-ai/) to develop large language models with realistic worst-case robustness guarantees ([News](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/news/2525-full.html)).

My research interests include (1) **AI Safety/Security**, including Robustness, Hallucinations, Safety Training, Jailbreaking, Adversarial ML. (2) **LLM Agents** and **Social Simulation**. (3) **AI+ Applications** in healthcare, robotics, business. My work has been published in top AI/ML venues such as ICLR, ICML, ACL, CVPR, IJCV, and featured by major media outlets including [Süddeutsche Zeitung](https://www.sueddeutsche.de/wissen/sprachmodelle-halluzinationen-o3-o4-mini-zuverlaessigkeit-luegen-li.3249515?reduced=true) and [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3291840/hong-kong-ai-model-proves-more-accurate-doctors-diagnosing-eye-conditions?module=perpetual_scroll_0&pgtype=article). I curate [a list of information points](https://treelli.github.io/aisafety/) to watch for AI Safety and Security.

Before joining Oxford, I completed my PhD in Machine Learning at the Department of Informatics, King’s College London, supervised by Prof. [Michael Spratling](https://mwspratling.codeberg.page/). During my PhD, I also interned at [Tencent’s Robotics X Lab](https://roboticsx.tencent.com/#/), working with Prof. [Lipeng Chen](https://lipeng-chen.github.io/) on enabling robots to learn throwing and catching skills from human demonstrations.

You can also find me on my [Oxford departmental profile](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/lin.li/) and the [OATML group profile](https://oatml.cs.ox.ac.uk/members/lin_li/).

I'm always open to collaborations and connections, so feel free to get in touch! I'm also looking for self-motivated students to explore exciting research directions together — if you're interested, reach out and let's grab a (cyber) ☕.
