Hello! I’m an AI researcher in the OATML Group at the Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford, working with Prof. Yarin Gal. I am also a Junior Research Fellow at Jesus College, Oxford (since October 2026). I lead a research stream on multimodal foundation model safety within the Horizon Europe project, DVPS. I am a Co-Principal Investigator on a research grant from Coefficient Giving to develop large language models with realistic worst-case robustness guarantees (News).

My research interests include (1) AI Safety/Security, including Robustness, Hallucinations, Safety Training, Jailbreaking, Adversarial ML. (2) LLM Agents and Social Simulation. (3) AI+ Applications in healthcare, robotics, business. My work has been published in top AI/ML venues such as ICLR, ICML, ACL, CVPR, IJCV, and featured by major media outlets including The Verge, Süddeutsche Zeitung and South China Morning Post. I curate a list of information points to watch for AI Safety and Security.

Before joining Oxford, I completed my PhD in Machine Learning at the Department of Informatics, King’s College London, supervised by Prof. Michael Spratling. During my PhD, I also interned at Tencent’s Robotics X Lab, working with Prof. Lipeng Chen on enabling robots to learn throwing and catching skills from human demonstrations.

You can also find me on my Oxford departmental profile and the OATML group profile.
